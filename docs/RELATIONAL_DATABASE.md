# Relational Database Design — NaeemSoft POS

## Design rules

- One physical shop and one stock balance per saleable product.
- Posted invoices are the source documents. Stock and account ledgers are derived in the same SQLite transaction.
- Never store a second editable stock balance in a cashier or customer/supplier table.
- A cash sale has `sales.customer_id = NULL`; a credit sale must reference a registered customer.
- Every supplier delivery line references a purchase product; `product_stock_links` maps that purchase product to the saleable stock product.
- Every stock movement generated by an invoice line references that exact `purchase_items.id` or `sale_items.id`.
- Supplier and customer account balances remain separate from stock quantity/value.

## Tables and their roles

| Table | Purpose | Key relationship |
|---|---|---|
| `products` | Product master; saleable and purchase-side records | `category_id` |
| `categories` | Product classification | one category to many products |
| `product_units` | Default units for each product | product ↔ units |
| `supplier_product_units` | Supplier-specific package name and weight | supplier ↔ product (junction) |
| `product_stock_links` | Explicit bridge from purchase product to saleable stock product | purchase product ↔ stock product |
| `customers` | Customer accounts | one customer to many credit sales |
| `customer_product_prices` | Customer-specific agreed price per product | customer ↔ product (junction) |
| `suppliers` | Supplier accounts | one supplier to many purchase invoices |
| `purchases` / `purchase_items` | Purchase invoice header and product lines | supplier → invoice → lines |
| `sales` / `sale_items` | Sales invoice header and product lines | optional customer → invoice → lines |
| `stock_movements` | Immutable stock movement ledger | product + exact source invoice line |
| `payments` | Payment event | parent of party and allocation junctions |\n| `customer_payment_links` / `supplier_payment_links` | Enforce payment-to-party foreign keys without polymorphic party references | payment ↔ one customer or supplier |\n| `sale_payment_allocations` / `purchase_payment_allocations` | Enforce payment-to-invoice foreign keys | payment ↔ sale or purchase invoice |
| `waste_records` | Spoilage/damage records | product |
| `returns` / `return_items` | Customer/supplier return documents | original invoice and product line |

## Posting flows

### Supplier purchase

1. Select supplier; supplier-specific units and package weights are loaded.
2. Save one purchase header and its multiple purchase lines.
3. For every line, resolve `purchase_product_id → product_stock_links → stock_product_id`.
4. Increase the saleable product's grams and inventory value; recalculate weighted-average cost.
5. Write one `stock_movements` row tied to the exact `purchase_item_id`.
6. Record supplier liability from the purchase total; record any immediate supplier settlement as a separate payment and allocation.

### Customer sale

1. Select a registered customer or cash customer.
2. Save the sale header and multiple sale lines.
3. Validate available stock before posting.
4. Decrease stock and inventory value using the weighted-average cost snapshot.
5. Write one stock movement tied to the exact `sale_item_id`.
6. For a registered customer, unpaid balance remains in that customer's account. Anonymous cash sales must be fully paid.

### Returns, waste and stock counts

- Returns must reference the original sale/purchase and original line where applicable, then create a compensating stock movement.
- Waste and count adjustments create explicit stock movement records; they do not silently overwrite the ledger.
- Every multi-table posting runs in one database transaction. If any validation or insert fails, all changes roll back.

## Integrity constraints

- One purchase product maps to at most one saleable stock product (`UNIQUE(purchase_product_id)`).
- Supplier package weight is unique per supplier + product + package name.
- Customer agreed price is unique per customer + product.
- A sale movement is unique per sale line; a purchase movement is unique per purchase line.
- Movement quantity is positive for inbound purchase and negative for outbound sale.
- Cash sale: no customer link and fully paid. Credit sale: customer link required.
- Historical invoice line values (package weight, package price, sale price, cost snapshot) are stored on the line and are not rewritten when product defaults change.

## Implementation status

Database schema version 13 adds expiry policy fields to products and an expiry index for purchase batches. Purchase posting creates a batch per invoice line when an expiry date is required; batch records reference the purchase line and the saleable stock product. Sales of expiry-controlled products consume valid batches in FEFO order and reject expired or insufficient valid quantities. Existing `paired_sale_product_id`, payment party references, and generic payment allocations are migrated into relational bridge tables when their target records exist. Existing invoice and account records are retained. Legacy polymorphic payment columns remain temporarily for compatibility; new payment postings write both legacy and typed junction rows.

Implemented in the current branch: supplier-specific package weights are loaded by the purchase cashier; customer-specific agreed prices are loaded by the sales cashier after selecting the customer; invoices, party statements, payment receipts and return vouchers have PDF preview/print/share paths; expiry alerts are available from administration.

Remaining work: multi-warehouse stock balances and transfers (the current stock ledger still represents one shop-wide balance), full product multi-unit/packaging editor, full Arabic/English/French localization and RTL/LTR switching, complete return/overpayment accounting tests, and month/two-month end-to-end business-cycle scenarios. Do not merge until these are implemented and the Android build/test pipeline passes.
